# Scrum Master Checklist

Comprehensive checklist for agile facilitation, sprint management, team coaching, and process improvement in AVANADE Method projects.

## 1. SPRINT PLANNING
- [ ] Sprint planning meeting scheduled and facilitated
- [ ] Product backlog refined and prioritized
- [ ] Sprint goal clearly defined and communicated
- [ ] Team capacity and velocity considered
- [ ] User stories estimated and committed
- [ ] Sprint backlog created and documented

## 2. DAILY STANDUPS
- [ ] Daily standup meetings facilitated
- [ ] Three questions format followed (done, doing, blockers)
- [ ] Impediments identified and tracked
- [ ] Team focus maintained on sprint goal
- [ ] Action items from standup documented
- [ ] Time-boxed to 15 minutes maximum

## 3. SPRINT EXECUTION
- [ ] Sprint progress tracked daily
- [ ] Burndown chart updated and reviewed
- [ ] Impediments actively removed
- [ ] Team collaboration facilitated
- [ ] Scope changes managed properly
- [ ] Risk mitigation strategies implemented

## 4. SPRINT REVIEW
- [ ] Sprint review meeting planned and facilitated
- [ ] Demo of completed features prepared
- [ ] Stakeholder feedback collected and documented
- [ ] Product backlog updated based on feedback
- [ ] Sprint metrics reviewed and analyzed
- [ ] Next sprint priorities discussed

## 5. SPRINT RETROSPECTIVE
- [ ] Retrospective meeting facilitated
- [ ] Team feedback on what went well collected
- [ ] Areas for improvement identified
- [ ] Action items for next sprint defined
- [ ] Team dynamics and morale assessed
- [ ] Process improvements implemented

## 6. BACKLOG MANAGEMENT
- [ ] Product backlog grooming sessions facilitated
- [ ] User stories refined with acceptance criteria
- [ ] Story dependencies identified and managed
- [ ] Definition of done maintained and enforced
- [ ] Backlog prioritization supported
- [ ] Epic breakdown facilitated

## 7. TEAM COACHING & DEVELOPMENT
- [ ] Team members coached on agile practices
- [ ] Agile principles and values reinforced
- [ ] Team self-organization encouraged
- [ ] Individual team member development supported
- [ ] Cross-functional collaboration promoted
- [ ] Continuous learning culture fostered

## 8. STAKEHOLDER COMMUNICATION
- [ ] Regular communication with Product Owner maintained
- [ ] Stakeholder expectations managed
- [ ] Sprint progress communicated to leadership
- [ ] Project status reports prepared and shared
- [ ] Risk and impediment escalation managed
- [ ] Change requests evaluated and communicated

## 9. METRICS & REPORTING
- [ ] Sprint velocity tracked and analyzed
- [ ] Team performance metrics collected
- [ ] Quality metrics monitored
- [ ] Burndown and burnup charts maintained
- [ ] Team satisfaction surveys conducted
- [ ] Process improvement metrics tracked

## 10. CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT
- [ ] Process inefficiencies identified and addressed
- [ ] Best practices documented and shared
- [ ] Team retrospective actions followed up
- [ ] Agile maturity assessment conducted
- [ ] Innovation and experimentation encouraged
- [ ] Knowledge sharing facilitated within team
