# Facilitate Brainstorming Session Task

Facilitate interactive brainstorming sessions with users. Be creative and adaptive in applying techniques.

## Process

### Step 1: Session Setup

Ask 4 context questions:

1. **Session Goal**: What specific outcome are you seeking?
2. **Participants**: Who is involved (roles, perspectives)?
3. **Scope**: What boundaries or focus areas should we establish?
4. **Constraints**: Any limitations or requirements to consider?

### Step 2: Technique Selection

Select appropriate brainstorming technique based on context:

**For New Ideas:**
- Classic Brainstorming
- SCAMPER Method
- Six Thinking Hats
- Random Word Association

**For Problem Solving:**
- Root Cause Analysis
- How Might We Questions
- Fishbone Diagram
- 5 Whys + Ideation

**For Innovation:**
- Blue Ocean Strategy
- Disruptive Innovation Canvas
- Assumption Reversal
- Future Back-casting

**For Process Improvement:**
- Value Stream Mapping + Ideation
- Pain Point Identification
- Opportunity Mapping
- Resource Optimization

### Step 3: Interactive Session

Facilitate the session using the selected technique:

#### Classic Brainstorming Flow:
1. **Warm-up** (5 min): Simple creative exercise
2. **Goal Alignment** (5 min): Clarify objectives
3. **Idea Generation** (20-30 min): No judgment, build on ideas
4. **Idea Clustering** (10 min): Group related concepts
5. **Evaluation** (15 min): Assess and prioritize
6. **Action Planning** (10 min): Next steps

#### SCAMPER Method Flow:
For each letter, ask targeted questions:
- **S**ubstitute: What can we replace or change?
- **C**ombine: What can we merge or integrate?
- **A**dapt: What can we copy or modify from elsewhere?
- **M**odify: What can we emphasize or minimize?
- **P**ut to other uses: How else can we use this?
- **E**liminate: What can we remove or simplify?
- **R**everse**: What can we rearrange or reverse?

### Step 4: Idea Development

For promising ideas, facilitate deeper exploration:

1. **Idea Expansion**: What if we took this further?
2. **Implementation Planning**: How might this work in practice?
3. **Risk Assessment**: What could go wrong?
4. **Resource Requirements**: What would we need?
5. **Success Metrics**: How would we measure success?

## Brainstorming Techniques Library

### Classic Techniques

**1. Traditional Brainstorming**
- No criticism during generation
- Wild ideas encouraged
- Build on others' ideas
- Aim for quantity over quality
- One conversation at a time

**2. Brainwriting**
- Silent idea generation first
- Write ideas before sharing
- Pass ideas for building upon
- Reduces social pressure
- Captures introverted input

**3. Mind Mapping**
- Start with central concept
- Branch out with related ideas
- Use colors and visuals
- Allow non-linear thinking
- Capture idea relationships

### Advanced Techniques

**1. Six Thinking Hats (Edward de Bono)**
- **White**: Facts and information
- **Red**: Emotions and feelings
- **Black**: Critical thinking
- **Yellow**: Positive thinking
- **Green**: Creative thinking
- **Blue**: Process control

**2. SCAMPER Method**
- Systematic creative thinking
- Each letter prompts different perspective
- Forces consideration of alternatives
- Good for product/service innovation

**3. Random Stimulus**
- Introduce random word/image
- Force connections to challenge
- Break conventional thinking
- Stimulate unexpected ideas

### Problem-Solving Techniques

**1. How Might We (HMW)**
- Reframe problems as opportunities
- Start with "How might we..."
- Generate multiple problem statements
- Focus on solutions not problems

**2. Assumption Reversal**
- List all assumptions
- Reverse each assumption
- Explore what becomes possible
- Challenge conventional wisdom

**3. Future Back-casting**
- Imagine ideal future state
- Work backwards to present
- Identify required steps
- Overcome current limitations

## Facilitation Best Practices

### Environment Setup
- Comfortable, inspiring space
- Plenty of wall space for visuals
- Sticky notes, markers, flip charts
- No distractions (phones, laptops)
- Energizing atmosphere

### Group Dynamics
- **Diverse Perspectives**: Include different roles/backgrounds
- **Psychological Safety**: Everyone feels safe to contribute
- **Equal Participation**: Ensure all voices are heard
- **Energy Management**: Take breaks, use energizers
- **Focus**: Keep discussions on track

### Common Challenges
- **Dominant Voices**: Use structured techniques to balance participation
- **Criticism Too Early**: Enforce "no judgment" rule firmly
- **Analysis Paralysis**: Set time limits, move to action
- **Status Quo Bias**: Use provocative questions to challenge assumptions
- **Implementation Fear**: Separate ideation from feasibility initially
